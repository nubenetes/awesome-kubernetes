## Nordic Sovereign Cloud and 3-AZ OpenStack Providers

- **(2026)** [UpCloud Sovereign European Cloud](https://upcloud.com/docs) 🌟 - Finnish-owned cloud infrastructure provider operating high-IOPS MaxIOPS NVMe VMs, Managed Kubernetes, Managed Databases, and an active Madrid (`es-mad1`) region alongside Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Helsinki, and Warsaw.
- **(2026)** [Elastx: Swedish 3-AZ Sovereign OpenStack Cloud](https://elastx.se/en) 🌟 - Swedish enterprise cloud running genuine 3-Availability-Zone OpenStack IaaS and Managed Kubernetes across three physically separated Stockholm data centers powered by 100% renewable hydro energy.
- **(2026)** [Cleura: Compliant European OpenStack Cloud](https://cleura.com) 🌟 - Swedish pioneer in open-source Sovereign Cloud Stack (OpenStack + Gardener Kubernetes) offering 3-AZ Swedish regions, Frankfurt/Karlskrona zones, DORA/NIS2 readiness, and strict European legal jurisdiction.
- **(2026)** [GleSYS Green European Cloud Infrastructure](https://glesys.com) 🌟 - Swedish independent sovereign cloud, bare metal, and colocation provider operating owned eco-friendly facilities in Falkenberg, Stockholm, and Oulu with full API/Terraform automation and zero US CLOUD Act ties.
- **(2026)** [Safespring Swedish and Norwegian Public-Sector Cloud](https://www.safespring.com) 🌟 - Nordic OpenStack cloud built specifically for European academia, municipalities, and public sector (**eSam / Schrems II** compliant), featuring fast automated S3/Compute APIs on 100% renewable energy.
- **(2026)** [evroc: European Sovereign Hyperscale Cloud Initiative](https://evroc.com) 🌟 - Stockholm-headquartered initiative engineering high-security, sustainable European hyperscale cloud and AI data center regions exclusively under EU jurisdiction.

## Hydro and Geothermal AI Data Centers and EuroHPC Supercomputing

- **(2026)** [Green Mountain Hydro-Powered Data Centers (Norway and UK)](https://greenmountain.no) 🌟 - World-renowned Norwegian ultra-low-PUE colocation operator running fjord-cooled and 100% hydro-powered AI and sovereign enterprise data centers (Stavanger, Rjukan, Oslo).
- **(2026)** [atNorth Nordic High-Density AI Data Centers](https://www.atnorth.com) 🌟 - Pan-Nordic high-density colocation and liquid-cooled GPU infrastructure operator spanning Iceland, Sweden, Finland, and Denmark with sub-1.15 PUE and geothermal/hydro electricity.
- **(2026)** [Verne Sustainable AI Data Centers (Iceland, Finland and UK)](https://www.verne.co) 🌟 - High-density HPC and AI colocation specialist running 100% geothermal and hydro campuses across Keflavik (Iceland) and Finland tailored for multi-megawatt LLM training clusters.
- **(2026)** [Bulk Infrastructure Nordic Green Data Centers](https://bulkinfrastructure.com) 🌟 - Norwegian hyperscale data center and dark-fiber subsea carrier operating Norway's massive renewable-powered NO1 campus and ultra-low-latency transatlantic/Nordic connectivity.
- **(2026)** [LUMI Pre-Exascale Supercomputer (CSC Kajaani Finland)](https://lumi-supercomputer.eu) 🌟 - One of Europe's cleanest pre-exascale EuroHPC supercomputers in Kajaani powered 100% by hydroelectricity with waste heat exported directly to the local municipal district heating grid.
