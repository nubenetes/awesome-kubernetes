## Global Neocloud Reference Leaders and Benchmarks

- **(2026)** [NVIDIA Cloud Partners (NCP) Ecosystem](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/gpu-cloud-computing/partners) 🌟 - Official NVIDIA directory and reference program for specialized GPU cloud providers building validated HGX/DGX H100, H200, Blackwell B200, and GB200 NVL72 AI clusters worldwide.
- **(2026)** [ClusterMAX: GPU Cloud Rating and Cluster Benchmarks](https://www.clustermax.ai) 🌟 - Independent engineering scorecard evaluating global Neoclouds and hyperscalers on NCCL multi-node fabric efficiency, Slurm and Kubernetes readiness, lifecycle automation, and effective goodput SLA.
- **(2026)** [MLCommons: MLPerf Training and Inference Benchmarks](https://mlcommons.org) 🌟 - Open engineering consortium publishing audited distributed training and LLM inference benchmarks across specialized AI clouds and hyperscalers.
- **(2026)** [Epoch AI: Compute Trends and AI Factory Economics](https://epoch.ai) 🌟 - Research institute tracking frontier training cluster scaling, power envelopes, accelerator FLOP/watt ratios, and capital efficiency across global AI data centers.
- **(2026)** [CoreWeave: Specialized GPU Hyperscaler](https://coreweave.com) 🌟 - Flagship North American NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud Partner operating bare-metal Kubernetes, SUNK (Slurm on Kubernetes), and InfiniBand NDR/X800 GPU fabrics across the US, UK, and Europe.
- **(2026)** [Nebius AI Cloud Platform](https://nebius.com) 🌟 - Amsterdam-headquartered European Neocloud building full-stack InfiniBand GPU clusters across Finland, France, the US, and UK with Managed Kubernetes (MK8s), Slurm, and Object Storage under European corporate governance.

## Sovereign Neoclouds vs Global Bare-Metal GPU Clouds

- **(2026)** [Nscale: European and UK Sovereign AI Hyperscaler](https://nscale.com) 🌟 - Vertically integrated British/European AI cloud building high-density renewable-powered GPU campuses with native Kubernetes, Terraform automation, and sovereign jurisdiction controls.
- **(2026)** [Crusoe Cloud: Clean-Energy AI Infrastructure](https://crusoe.ai) 🌟 - Energy-first AI cloud co-locating gigawatt-scale NVIDIA and AMD GPU clusters at curtailed renewable and stranded energy sites across North America and Iceland.
- **(2026)** [Lambda AI Cloud](https://lambda.ai) 🌟 - Deep-learning specialized GPU cloud and cluster engineering provider offering 1-Click Clusters, multi-node InfiniBand HGX/Blackwell systems, and transparent hourly/reserved pricing.
- **(2026)** [Scaleway GPU Instances and AI Cloud](https://www.scaleway.com/en/gpu-instances) 🌟 - French Iliad Group cloud provider pairing GDPR/EU AI Act data sovereignty with elastic H100/L40S/Blackwell GPUs, Kapsule Kubernetes, and Managed Inference endpoints.
- **(2026)** [Verda (formerly DataCrunch): European Renewable GPU Cloud](https://verda.com) 🌟 - Helsinki-headquartered European Neocloud running hydro- and wind-powered NVIDIA B200, H200, and H100 clusters across Finland and Iceland under EU/EEA jurisdiction.
- **(2026)** [Baseten: Dedicated GPU Inference Cloud](https://www.baseten.co) 🌟 - High-performance inference Neocloud combining Truss model packaging, TensorRT-LLM compilation, and multi-region autoscaling across dedicated H100/H200/B200 clusters.
- **(2026)** [Modal: Serverless Python GPU Cloud](https://modal.com) 🌟 - Sub-second cold-start serverless GPU platform enabling Python-defined distributed training, batch inference, and sandboxes across H100, H200, and Blackwell fleets.
