- **(2026)** [OVHcloud Managed Kubernetes Service (MKS)](https://www.ovhcloud.com/en-ie/public-cloud/kubernetes/) 🌟 - CNCF-certified Managed Kubernetes with free multi-master control plane, Cinder/Octavia OpenStack integration, NVIDIA H100/L40S worker pools, and multi-AZ support in Paris.
- **(2026)** [OVHcloud Help and Technical Documentation](https://help.ovhcloud.com/csm/en-home) 🌟 - Technical knowledge base covering vRack L2 private networking, OVH KMS/IAM, MKS lifecycle, and S3 Object Lock IMMUTABLE backups.
- **(2026)** [Scaleway Cloud Platform (Iliad Group)](https://github.com/scaleway) 🌟 - French developer-first sovereign cloud offering 3-AZ Paris/Amsterdam/Warsaw regions, Kubernetes Kapsule and multi-cloud Kosmos, Managed Databases, and one of Europe's largest public catalogs of NVIDIA H100/B200/L4 GPUs and Serverless AI Endpoints.
- **(2026)** [Scaleway Engineering Blog](https://www.scaleway.com/en/blog/rss.xml) 🌟 - Official engineering feed covering Scaleway's sovereign cloud architecture, European AI Factories, Kubernetes Kapsule upgrades, and EU Data Act compliance.
- **(2026)** [3DS OUTSCALE (Dassault Systemes)](https://en.outscale.com/) 🌟 - First French IaaS provider with full-scope ANSSI SecNumCloud 3.2 qualification across three French regions, offering AWS-like Cockpit/API semantics for ultra-regulated defense, healthcare, and public-sector workloads.
- **(2026)** [Clever Cloud Sovereign PaaS](https://www.clever.cloud/) 🌟 - French European-owned Platform-as-a-Service (Nantes) automating zero-ops application runtimes, managed PostgreSQL/Redis add-ons, SecNumCloud-aligned deployments, and multi-cloud/on-prem Materia clusters.
- **(2026)** [Cloud Temple Sovereign IaaS](https://www.cloud-temple.com/en/) 🌟 - French SecNumCloud-certified trusted IaaS specializing in critical enterprise workloads, sovereign OpenShift/Kubernetes hosting, and ultra-regulated healthcare/banking environments.
- **(2026)** [Bleu Trusted Cloud (Capgemini and Orange)](https://www.bleucloud.fr/en/) 🌟 - Independent French company operating sovereign Azure-stack and Microsoft 365 environments within France to address SecNumCloud 3.2 criteria for public administrations.
- **(2026)** [S3NS Trusted Cloud (Thales and Google)](https://www.s3ns.io/en) 🌟 - French trusted-cloud joint venture coupling Thales sovereign operational control and encryption custody with Google Cloud infrastructure.
- **(2026)** [Mistral AI Sovereign Frontier Models](https://mistral.ai/) 🌟 - Paris-based European AI champion delivering open-weight (Mistral/Mixtral/Codestral) and enterprise models optimized for self-hosted Kubernetes vLLM and European sovereign cloud AI endpoints.
