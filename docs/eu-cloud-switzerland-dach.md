## Switzerland and Austria Sovereign Cloud Providers

- **(2026)** [Exoscale: Swiss and European Sovereign Cloud](https://www.exoscale.com) 🌟 - Swiss/Austrian sovereign IaaS and SKS Managed Kubernetes provider (A1 Group) operating GDPR-shielded zones across Zurich, Geneva, Frankfurt, Munich, Vienna, and Sofia with strict Swiss privacy governance and zero US ownership.
- **(2026)** [Infomaniak Public Cloud (Switzerland)](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/hosting/public-cloud) 🌟 - Swiss independent OpenStack public cloud emphasizing ecological hydro-powered data centers, zero US CLOUD Act exposure, and transparent Pay-as-You-Go billing.
- **(2026)** [cloudscale.ch Swiss IaaS and Managed OpenStack Cloud](https://www.cloudscale.ch/en) 🌟 - 100% Swiss-owned and operated OpenStack cloud across two physically separated locations in Zurich and Lupfig, offering FINMA-friendly transparency, Terraform parity, and zero cross-border data export.
- **(2026)** [Swisscom Enterprise Sovereign Cloud](https://www.swisscom.ch/en/business/enterprise.html) 🌟 - Switzerland's flagship enterprise ICT and telecommunications provider delivering Swiss-jurisdiction private clouds, FINMA-compliant Managed OpenShift/Kubernetes, and geo-redundant Swiss banking enclaves.
- **(2026)** [VSHN: Swiss Cloud Native and APPUiO Managed OpenShift](https://www.vshn.ch/en) 🌟 - Zurich-based DevOps and CNCF Kubernetes/OpenShift managed service specialist delivering multi-cloud APPUiO platform operations strictly hosted across Swiss sovereign providers (cloudscale.ch, Exoscale).
- **(2026)** [APPUiO Managed Swiss OpenShift Platform](https://www.appuio.ch) 🌟 - Enterprise Managed OpenShift Platform-as-a-Service engineered in Switzerland enabling regulated Swiss enterprises to deploy containerized apps with strict Swiss data residency.
- **(2026)** [Anexia World Wide Cloud (Austrian HQ)](https://anexia.com/en) 🌟 - Austrian-headquartered enterprise cloud and Kubernetes provider (Klagenfurt and Vienna) delivering ISO 27001 / EU-sovereign IaaS, ANX Cloud Terraform automation, and strict DACH-CEE operational control.

## Alpine Private Colocation, Interconnection and Supercomputing

- **(2026)** [Green Datacenter Switzerland (Zurich and Lupfig Campuses)](https://www.green.ch/en) 🌟 - High-efficiency Swiss Tier III/IV colocation campuses supporting dual-site metro synchronization, renewable hydro power, and liquid-cooled high-density AI cages.
- **(2026)** [Equinix Switzerland IBX Data Centers (Zurich and Geneva)](https://www.equinix.com/data-centers/europe-colocation/switzerland-colocation) 🌟 - Carrier-neutral Swiss colocation facilities serving Zurich private banking and Geneva international organizations with direct Fabric on-ramps.
- **(2026)** [SwissIX: Neutral Swiss Internet Exchange](https://www.swissix.ch) 🌟 - Switzerland's primary neutral peering exchange connecting Swiss banks, sovereign clouds (`cloudscale.ch`, `Exoscale`, `Infomaniak`), and European transit networks in Zurich.
- **(2026)** [Vienna Internet Exchange (VIX Austria)](https://www.vix.at/en) 🌟 - Central Europe's key neutral IXP in Vienna anchoring Austrian domestic cloud traffic and high-availability interconnection across DACH and Central-Eastern Europe.
- **(2026)** [CSCS Alps Supercomputer (Lugano Switzerland)](https://www.cscs.ch/computers/alps) 🌟 - Swiss National Supercomputing Centre flagship NVIDIA Grace Hopper (GH200) system powering sovereign Swiss climate, biomedical, and open-weight AI research (Swiss AI Initiative).

## Swiss FINMA, FADP and Neutrality Compliance

- **(2026)** [FINMA: Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority](https://www.finma.ch/en) 🌟 - Official Swiss financial regulator governing bank/insurer cloud outsourcing (Circular 2018/3 Operational Risks and Critical Data Residency audits) requiring clear Swiss legal auditability and tested exit plans.
- **(2026)** [Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (Revised FADP / revDSG)](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/en) 🌟 - Official Swiss Confederation data-protection statute governing cross-border data transfers, privacy-by-default controls, and sovereign Swiss data custody.
