## Large-Scale Bare-Metal Training Neoclouds

- **(2026)** [CoreWeave Cloud Platform and Documentation](https://docs.coreweave.com) 🌟 - Pioneer US Neocloud engineered around non-blocking InfiniBand fabrics, Kubernetes-native GPU scheduling (CKS), Slurm on Kubernetes (SUNK), and multi-thousand-GPU NVIDIA Blackwell pods.
- **(2026)** [Lambda Cloud and 1-Click Clusters](https://lambda.ai) 🌟 - San Jose AI compute specialist offering reserved NVIDIA HGX/Blackwell InfiniBand clusters, deep-learning stack images, and clean per-hour pricing vs AWS/Azure.
- **(2026)** [Crusoe Cloud Documentation and AI Clusters](https://docs.crusoe.ai) 🌟 - Climate-aligned North American Neocloud converting stranded and renewable energy into high-density NVIDIA H100/H200/GB200 and AMD MI300X capacity backed by official Terraform automation.
- **(2026)** [TensorWave: AMD Instinct MI300X and MI325X Neocloud](https://tensorwave.com) 🌟 - Pure-play US Neocloud specializing in high-HBM-memory AMD Instinct MI300X/MI325X bare-metal clusters tuned for 70B–405B+ long-context LLM training and inference on ROCm.
- **(2026)** [Voltage Park: Flat-Rate Non-Profit-Backed GPU Cloud](https://voltagepark.com) 🌟 - US wholesale GPU Neocloud deploying tens of thousands of NVIDIA H100 accelerators on low-cost bare-metal Kubernetes and InfiniBand at predictable flat rates.
- **(2026)** [Applied Digital: North Dakota AI Factory Campuses](https://www.applieddigital.com) 🌟 - Purpose-built high-density liquid-cooled AI data centers across North Dakota providing hundreds of megawatts of low-cost cold-climate US GPU hosting.
- **(2026)** [IREN: Renewable-Powered AI Cloud Services](https://iren.com) 🌟 - Vertically integrated US and Canadian data center operator transforming 100% renewable grid-connected substations in Texas and British Columbia into H100/H200 and Blackwell clusters.

## Serverless Inference, Elastic GPU and Canadian Green AI Hubs

- **(2026)** [Together AI: Optimized Inference and Fine-Tuning Cloud](https://www.together.ai) 🌟 - US research-backed AI cloud pairing speculative decoding and FlashAttention kernels with dedicated H100/H200 clusters and sub-millisecond open-weight inference APIs.
- **(2026)** [Fireworks AI: Production Compound Inference Platform](https://fireworks.ai) 🌟 - Low-latency serverless and dedicated GPU inference cloud running quantized open-weight models, function calling, and structured JSON pipelines at fraction-of-hyperscaler cost.
- **(2026)** [RunPod: Global Pod and Serverless GPU Cloud](https://www.runpod.io) 🌟 - Developer-friendly elastic GPU cloud providing instant containers, Secure Cloud data centers, Network Volumes, and autoscale vLLM Serverless endpoints.
- **(2026)** [Vultr Cloud GPU Documentation (NVIDIA Certified)](https://docs.vultr.com/products/compute/instances/cloud-gpu) 🌟 - Official Vultr technical reference for global Cloud GPU VMs and bare-metal accelerators paired with official Terraform provisioning and predictable global bandwidth pools.
- **(2026)** [Hyperstack (NexGen Cloud US and EU)](https://www.hyperstack.cloud) 🌟 - On-demand NVIDIA H100/L40S/A100 Neocloud offering fast VM hibernation, high-speed NVMe ephemeral storage, and clean Terraform/API orchestration across Canada, US, and Europe.
- **(2026)** [DigitalOcean GPU Droplets and Bare Metal](https://www.digitalocean.com/products/gpu-droplets) 🌟 - Simple developer cloud delivering predictable per-hour NVIDIA H100/RTX GPU Droplets and Managed Kubernetes worker pools (integrating Paperspace heritage).
- **(2026)** [QScale High-Density Computing Campuses (Quebec, Canada)](https://qscale.com) 🌟 - Clean Canadian hydro-powered supercomputing and AI campus operator in Levis (Quebec) pairing zero-water cooling with circular greenhouse heat recovery under PIPEDA/Quebec Law 25 governance.
