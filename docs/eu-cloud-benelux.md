## Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg Sovereign Cloud and AI Providers

- **(2026)** [Nebius Sovereign AI Cloud (Amsterdam HQ)](https://nebius.com) 🌟 - European-headquartered AI hyperscaler operating massive InfiniBand-interconnected NVIDIA H100, H200, and Blackwell B200 superclusters in Finland and France with Kubernetes (MK8s), Slurm, and Sovereign S3 storage.
- **(2026)** [Gcore Cloud and Edge AI (Luxembourg HQ)](https://gcore.com) 🌟 - European edge network and AI cloud headquartered in Luxembourg delivering low-latency global PoPs, Managed Kubernetes, bare metal, and European GPU inference nodes.
- **(2026)** [Leaseweb Bare Metal, Public Cloud and Private DCs (Amsterdam HQ)](https://www.leaseweb.com) 🌟 - Global Dutch-headquartered IaaS powerhouse operating high-throughput Amsterdam and Frankfurt facilities with single-tenant bare metal, CloudStack/VMware private clouds, Terraform automation, and generous bandwidth allowances.
- **(2026)** [Cyso Cloud Sovereign Dutch OpenStack and Kubernetes](https://cyso.com) 🌟 - Dutch sovereign public cloud (Alkmaar) powered 100% by European OpenStack and Managed Kubernetes, offering ISO 27001 / NEN 7510 / BIO compliance for regulated healthcare and SaaS workloads.
- **(2026)** [Leafcloud Sustainable European Kubernetes and GPU Cloud](https://leafcloud.com) 🌟 - Amsterdam-based green OpenStack and Managed Kubernetes cloud installing compute nodes directly inside urban district heating loops to eliminate traditional data-center cooling waste.
- **(2026)** [TransIP Euro-Compliant VPS and Cloud Storage (team.blue)](https://www.transip.eu) 🌟 - Netherlands-based European IaaS operator (part of team.blue) providing predictable EUR-billed VPS, HA-IP load balancing, private networks, and Dutch data residency for developers and agencies.
- **(2026)** [Proximus NXT Sovereign Cloud and Telco IT (Belgium and Luxembourg)](https://www.proximusnxt.be/en) 🌟 - Belgian enterprise sovereign ICT and cloud champion delivering multi-DC private clouds, SecNumCloud/DORA-aligned banking platforms, and NATO/EU institutional connectivity across Brussels and Luxembourg.

## FLAP-D Colocation, AMS-IX Interconnection and Luxembourg Tier IV Data Centers

- **(2026)** [AMS-IX: Amsterdam Internet Exchange](https://www.ams-ix.net/ams) 🌟 - One of the world's largest non-profit neutral peering exchanges anchored in Amsterdam, providing ultra-low-latency European and transatlantic traffic distribution across sovereign clouds and carriers.
- **(2026)** [LuxConnect Sovereign Tier IV Data Centers (Luxembourg)](https://www.luxconnect.lu) 🌟 - State-backed Luxembourg Tier IV data center operator (Bettembourg and Bissen) offering ultra-secure dark-fiber loops, data-embassy resilience, and 100% renewable power for DORA financial institutions.
- **(2026)** [NorthC Regional Dutch and Benelux Data Centers](https://www.northcdatacenters.com/en) 🌟 - Regional carrier-neutral data center network across Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Eindhoven, Brussels, and Germany supporting regional edge recovery and strict local data residency.
- **(2026)** [Equinix Amsterdam IBX Campuses (Science Park and Schiphol)](https://www.equinix.com/data-centers/europe-colocation/netherlands-colocation/amsterdam-data-centers) 🌟 - Tier III+ colocation hub directly adjacent to AMS-IX and NL-ix providing high-density interconnection for financial, cloud-native, and multi-region European architectures.
- **(2026)** [Digital Realty Amsterdam Science Park Campus](https://www.digitalrealty.com/data-centers/emea/amsterdam) 🌟 - Carrier-neutral enterprise colocation cluster offering direct AMS-IX peering, renewable PPAs, and sovereign private-cloud cages inside Amsterdam's interconnection core.
