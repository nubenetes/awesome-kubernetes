### Benelux Interconnection Core (AMS-IX, FLAP-D and Luxembourg DORA)

The Benelux triangle anchors both **Europe's highest-capacity Internet peering** and **continental Europe's premier AI &amp; DORA financial ecosystems**:

- **[AMS-IX (Amsterdam)](https://www.ams-ix.net/ams) FLAP-D Dominance:** Amsterdam sits alongside Frankfurt, London, and Paris as a core European backbone hub, hosting dense [**Equinix Amsterdam**](https://www.equinix.com/data-centers/europe-colocation/netherlands-colocation/amsterdam-data-centers), [**Digital Realty Amsterdam**](https://www.digitalrealty.com/data-centers/emea/amsterdam), [**NorthC**](https://www.northcdatacenters.com/en), and [**Leaseweb**](https://www.leaseweb.com) footprints (represented by the [**Dutch Data Center Association**](https://www.dutchdatacenters.nl)) with sub-5 ms proximity to Belgium and Western Germany.
- **Home to Europe's Flagship AI Hyperscaler ([Nebius](https://nebius.com)):** Headquartered in **Amsterdam**, [**Nebius**](https://nebius.com) leads Europe in mega-scale InfiniBand-interconnected **NVIDIA H100, H200, and Blackwell B200** cloud superclusters across Finland and France — delivering CoreWeave/AWS `p5` parity under European corporate leadership.
- **Dutch Sector Standards ([BIO](https://www.bio-overheid.nl) &amp; [NEN 7510](https://www.nen.nl/en/certificatie-en-keurmerken-nen-7510)) + Heat Reuse:** Dutch public institutions enforce **BIO** and healthcare requires **NEN 7510**, met natively by OpenStack champions [**Cyso Cloud**](https://cyso.com) and [**Leafcloud**](https://leafcloud.com).
- **Luxembourg Tier IV Data Embassies ([LuxConnect](https://www.luxconnect.lu) &amp; [Gcore](https://gcore.com)):** Built for European banking, investment funds ([**CSSF**](https://www.cssf.lu/en) / [**DORA**](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj)), and sovereign state "data embassies," Luxembourg combines dual-site **LuxConnect Tier IV** resilience with dark-fiber loops into Brussels, Paris, and Frankfurt.
